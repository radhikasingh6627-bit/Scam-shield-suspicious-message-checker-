      How It Works

      1

      Paste

      Enter any suspicious text. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

      2

      Match rules

      Regular-expression rules look for patterns such as urgency, OTP requests, short links and fee demands.

      3

      Score

      Each rule has a weight. Related rules are grouped so repeated words don't inflate the score. Under 30 is Low, 30–59 Medium, 60+ High.

      Safety Tips

      🔐 Guard your OTP

      Banks never ask you to share OTPs, PINs or passwords.

      🔗 Inspect links

      Avoid shortened or odd-looking links. Type the official website yourself.

      ⏳ Slow down

      Scammers create panic. Pause and verify through an official number.

      🚫 No fees for prizes

      Real rewards never require you to pay a fee first.

      About & Limitations

      ScamShield is a first-year B.Tech CSE college demo built with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. It uses a transparent, rule-based scoring system, not machine learning or artificial intelligence.

      • A scam can score Low if it uses unusual wording or other languages.
      • A genuine message can score higher if it contains words like "bank" or "OTP".
      • A Low score never means a message is safe. Always verify with the official source.
      • It does not check whether a link is actually malicious.
